AI Assistant Complete Workflow for a Multi-Vendor E-commerce Platform

Overview

The AI Assistant is the central intelligent assistant of the marketplace. It acts as a shopping assistant, customer support agent, sales representative, vendor assistant, and super admin assistant, available 24/7. It can answer questions, perform tasks (with user confirmation where necessary), automate workflows, and provide business insights.

---

1. Customer Workflow

A. Welcome & Onboarding

- Greet customers with a friendly welcome.
- Introduce available services.
- Offer quick action buttons such as:
  - Find Products
  - Track Order
  - Contact Support
  - Become a Vendor
  - Today's Deals
  - My Orders

Example:

«👋 Welcome to Africart! I'm your AI Shopping Assistant. I can help you find products, compare prices, place orders, track deliveries, answer questions, and connect you with our support team.»

---

B. Understand Customer Intent

The AI detects what the customer wants, including:

- Search for products
- Compare products
- Place an order
- Track an order
- Cancel an order
- Return or refund
- Delivery information
- Payment help
- Contact seller
- Contact Super Admin
- Become a vendor
- Report a problem
- Manage account
- Find promotions

---

C. Product Search

The AI searches across all vendors.

Customers can search by:

- Product name
- Category
- Brand
- Price
- Vendor
- Color
- Size
- Location
- Rating
- Discount
- Availability

Example:

Customer:

«I need wireless earbuds under GHS 300.»

AI:

- Searches every vendor
- Finds matching products
- Shows ratings
- Shows delivery estimates
- Shows available discounts

---

D. Product Recommendations

The AI asks questions before recommending.

Example:

Customer:

«I need a laptop for programming.»

AI asks:

- Budget?
- Preferred brand?
- New or used?
- Minimum RAM?
- Preferred storage?

Then recommends the best products.

---

E. Product Comparison

Compare products by:

- Price
- Rating
- Reviews
- Specifications
- Warranty
- Vendor reputation
- Delivery time

The AI also explains which option is better based on the customer's needs.

F. Smart Recommendations

The AI suggests:

- Cheaper alternatives

G. Product Questions

Customers can ask:

- Is this original?
- Is there warranty?
- Does it support fast charging?
- Is it waterproof?
- Is it in stock?
- What's the difference between these models?

The AI answers using product information.

H. Image-Based Shopping

Customers upload a picture.

The AI:

- Identifies the product
- Finds similar products
- Suggests cheaper alternatives
- Finds matching accessories

---

I. Cart Management

The AI can:

- Add products
- Remove products
- Update quantity
- Save for later
- Move items to wishlist
- Recommend additional products
- Suggest coupons

---

J. Checkout Assistant

The AI:

- Reviews the cart
- Confirms delivery address
- Estimates shipping
- Calculates taxes
- Applies valid coupons
- Confirms payment method
- Reviews final total

---

K. AI Order Placement

Customers can tell the AI:

«Order this phone.»

The AI:

- Adds the item to the cart
- Reviews the order
- Confirms delivery details
- Confirms payment method
- Displays total amount
- Requests final confirmation
- Automatically places the order after confirmation

Customers can also say:

- Buy it now
- Reorder my last purchase
- Order the cheapest one
- Order two of these

---

L. Delivery Assistance

- Estimate delivery time
- Track orders
- Notify customers of delays
- Change delivery address before shipping
- Contact delivery partners when appropriate

- Add products
- Remove products

- Notify when products are back in stock
